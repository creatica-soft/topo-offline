# Topo Offline support

## Getting help

Report a problem or suggest an improvement by opening an issue at
[github.com/creatica-soft/topo-offline/issues](https://github.com/creatica-soft/topo-offline/issues).
Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and, for map problems, roughly where you were
looking.

## Common questions

**How do I use maps without phone signal?**
Before you go, move the map to where you'll be walking and tap **Download area**. The app saves
the topo map, place names, walking tracks and elevation data for that area. Out there, tap
**Online** to switch to **Offline** (or just carry on: saved maps are used automatically).

**Which areas have detailed topo maps?**
New South Wales, Queensland, Tasmania and New Zealand. Elsewhere in Australia the app shows
Geoscience Australia's 1:250,000 national map, and in Victoria it draws walking tracks over it.

**Why does a campsite look different (teal)?**
Teal campsites come from OpenStreetMap, where volunteers add them. They're very useful but may be
out of date. Green campsites come from park agencies.

**Are closures complete?**
No. Closures are shown for Victorian state forests and New Zealand DOC land only. Always check
the park agency's alerts before you go.

**How much space do downloads use?**
The download screen shows an estimate before you start. A 20 × 20 km area is usually 70–350 MB,
depending on the state. Delete areas under **Offline maps** (swipe left).

**Does the app track me or record my walks?**
No. See the [privacy policy](../privacy/). The app doesn't record tracks, which also keeps battery
use low.

## Safety

Maps and data can be out of date or wrong. Always carry a paper map and compass and a way to call
for help, tell someone your plans, and check park alerts and the weather before you go.
