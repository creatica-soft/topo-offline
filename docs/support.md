# Topo Offline support

## Getting help

Report a problem or suggest an improvement by opening an issue at
[github.com/creatica-soft/topo-offline/issues](https://github.com/creatica-soft/topo-offline/issues).
Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and, for map problems, roughly where you were
looking.

## Common questions

**How do I use maps without phone signal?**
Before you go, move the map to where you'll be walking and tap **Download**. The app saves
the topo map, place names, walking tracks and elevation data for that area. Out there, tap
**Online** to switch to **Offline** (or just carry on: saved maps are used automatically).

**Which areas have detailed topo maps?**
New South Wales, Queensland, Tasmania, New Zealand, the United States, Canada, Switzerland and
Norway. Elsewhere in Australia the app shows Geoscience Australia's 1:250,000 national map, and in
Victoria it draws walking tracks over it.

**How do I choose the map or switch to miles and feet?**
Tap the gear at the top left of the map. Settings has the maps by country, units, recording,
and the privacy policy and map credits. The map also switches by itself when you move into another
state or country.

**Why does a campsite look different (teal)?**
Teal campsites come from OpenStreetMap, where volunteers add them. They're very useful but may be
out of date. Green campsites come from park agencies.

**Are closures complete?**
No. Closures are shown for Victorian state forests, New Zealand DOC land and US Forest Service
campgrounds only. Always check the park agency's alerts before you go.

**How much space do downloads use?**
The download screen shows an estimate before you start. A 20 × 20 km area is usually 70–350 MB,
depending on the state. Delete areas under **Offline maps** (swipe left).

**Does the app track me or record my walks?**
The app never tracks you or sends your location anywhere; see the [privacy policy](../privacy/).
Recording is off unless you turn it on in Settings, and recordings stay on your iPhone.
Recording keeps GPS on while the phone is locked, so it uses more battery. For a long break,
tap Pause to turn GPS off, and Resume when you set off again.

**How does the app know when I stopped?**
Staying within 50 m of one spot for 5 minutes counts as a stop: it's left out of moving time and
average speed, and GPS wandering there (it drifts by tens of metres indoors) isn't drawn or added
to the distance. If you forget to pause, a notification reminds you: press and hold it for a Pause button
(it works on the lock screen). Change the distance
and time in Settings › Recording › Stops: a larger distance or shorter time catches more stops
(pottering about at lunch or camp), but very slow walking could then count as stopped. Every
recorded point is kept, so a new setting also applies to past recordings.

**Can I follow a track as well as my own routes?**
Yes. Tap a track on the map, then Follow on its card. Tracks published in several pieces are
joined into one line for the distance done and to go; side tracks don't count as off route.

**Will Follow warn me with the phone locked?**
Yes. While you follow a route, GPS stays on with the phone locked, and if you stray more than 100 m
from the route you get a notification with sound saying how far away the route is and in which
direction. Change the distance in Settings › Following a route, or by tapping the banner at the
top of the map. Allow notifications when the app asks (or later in Settings › Topo Offline ›
Notifications). Following keeps GPS on like recording does, so tap Stop following when you're done.

## Safety

Maps and data can be out of date or wrong. Always carry a paper map and compass and a way to call
for help, tell someone your plans, and check park alerts and the weather before you go.
