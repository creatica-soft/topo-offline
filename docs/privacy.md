# Topo Offline privacy policy

*Effective 12 November 2026*

**Short version:** Topo Offline doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account,
no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no server of its own. Your location
stays on your phone.

## What the app does not do

- It doesn't ask you to sign up or log in.
- It doesn't collect, store or send any personal information to us. We don't operate any server
  that the app talks to.
- It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code from anyone.
- It doesn't track you across apps or websites, and doesn't use your data for advertising.

## Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map. Your
location is used only on your phone and is never sent anywhere by the app. You can turn location
access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

## Map and data requests

To show maps, the app requests map images and related data directly from public data services,
mostly government agencies:

- Spatial Services NSW, the Queensland Government, the LIST (Tasmania), Geoscience Australia,
  the Victorian Government (Vicmap and DEECA) and NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service
- Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (LINZ) and the New Zealand Department of
  Conservation
- Terrain Tiles on Amazon Web Services (elevation data)

Some of these services are hosted on Esri ArcGIS Online or Amazon Web Services. Like any website,
a service receives the technical information needed to answer a request: your device's internet
address and which map area or place name was requested. When you search for a place in
Australia, the text you type is sent to Geoscience Australia's place-name service. New Zealand
place names are searched on your phone. The app sends nothing else, and no identifier that links
requests to you. How each service handles its own server logs is set out in its own privacy
policy.

You can switch the app to **Offline** mode, and it will make no requests at all and use only maps
and data already on your phone.

The app is built with Apple's iOS and MapKit frameworks, which are covered by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Data stored on your phone

Maps and data you download and your saved areas are stored only on your phone. They aren't
included in iCloud backups and aren't shared. Your settings (such as the map you last chose) are
stored like any app's settings and are included in your own device backups. Delete a saved area
in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

## Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
