# Topo Offline privacy policy

*Effective 18 November 2026*

**Short version:** Topo Offline doesn't collect any information about you. There's no account,
no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app has no server of its own. Your location
stays on your phone.

## What the app does not do

- It doesn't ask you to sign up or log in.
- It doesn't collect, store or send any personal information to us. We don't operate any server
  that the app talks to.
- It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code from anyone.
- It doesn't track you across apps or websites, and doesn't use your data for advertising.

## Your location

If you allow it, the app uses your phone's location to show where you are on the map, and to
show your progress along a route you're following. Your location is used only on your phone and
is never sent anywhere by the app. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings ›
Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Recording is off unless you turn it on. While you're recording a walk or ride or following a route,
the app keeps using your location when the phone is locked or another app is open, and iOS shows a
location indicator while it does. This stops when you tap Stop or Stop following. Recordings are
saved only on your phone. The warning when you stray from a route you're following, and the
reminder to pause a recording that looks stopped, are notifications created on your phone, without
sending anything over the internet.

## Map and data requests

To show maps, the app requests map images and related data directly from public data services,
mostly government agencies:

- Spatial Services NSW, the Queensland Government, the LIST (Tasmania), Geoscience Australia,
  the Victorian Government (Vicmap and DEECA) and NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service
- Toitū Te Whenua Land Information New Zealand (LINZ) and the New Zealand Department of
  Conservation
- the U.S. Geological Survey (The National Map) and the USDA Forest Service
- Natural Resources Canada, Parks Canada, the governments of British Columbia and Ontario, swisstopo (the Swiss Federal Office of Topography, via geo.admin.ch)
  and Kartverket (the Norwegian Mapping Authority, via Geonorge)
- Terrain Tiles on Amazon Web Services (elevation data)

Some of these services are hosted on Esri ArcGIS Online or Amazon Web Services. Like any website,
a service receives the technical information needed to answer a request: your device's internet
address and which map area or place name was requested. When you search for a place,
the text you type is sent to the place-name service of the country the map is showing:
Geoscience Australia, the U.S. Geological Survey, Natural Resources Canada, swisstopo or
Kartverket. New Zealand place names are searched on your phone. Each request also names the app and its website, the same for every user. The app sends nothing else, and no identifier that links requests to you. How each service handles its own server logs is set out in its own privacy
policy.

You can switch the app to **Offline** mode, and it will make no requests at all and use only maps
and data already on your phone.

Where no topographic map is available for the area on screen (for example outside the countries
the app covers), the app shows Apple's map instead, which loads map images from Apple. This doesn't happen in
Offline mode. The app is built with Apple's iOS and MapKit frameworks, which are covered by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Data stored on your phone

Maps and data you download and your saved areas are stored only on your phone. They aren't
included in iCloud backups and aren't shared. Your routes and recordings are also stored only
on your phone. They are included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer), like any app's
documents. They leave the phone only if you share them as a GPX file yourself. A GPX file you
import is copied into the app and isn't sent anywhere. Your settings (such as the map you last chose) are
stored like any app's settings and are included in your own device backups. Delete a saved area
in the app, or delete the app to remove everything.

## Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.
